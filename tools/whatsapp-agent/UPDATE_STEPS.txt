WhatsApp capture agent v2 - update steps for the office PC (2026-10-23)

1. On the office PC, open the folder  C:\Users\info\Documents\WhatsAppMonitor
2. Close the agent window if it is running (the black console that says "WhatsApp capture agent running").
3. Rename the current  wa_agent.py  to  wa_agent_v1.py  (keep it, just in case).
4. Copy the new  wa_agent.py  from  OneDrive (TS)\CLAUDE\WhatsAppMonitor  into the folder.
   Keep your existing  wa_agent.config.json  as it is (same URL, same secret).
5. Double-click  start_wa_agent.bat  as before.
   The window must say:  "WhatsApp capture agent v2.0 running."
   and then a line like:  "startup: 23 notifications waiting, 9 WhatsApp messages forwarded"
6. Open the CRM > WhatsApp. At the top you will see
   "WhatsApp PC ts-claude  running · last message ..."  - that line is the heartbeat.

What changes:
- Messages still waiting in the Windows notification center are forwarded when the agent starts
  (up to 48 hours back). The CRM ignores repeats, so nothing doubles.
- New toasts are caught within half a second, so a message read on a phone right away is still captured.
- Every minute the agent tells the CRM it is alive. If it stops, or forwards nothing for two hours
  in business hours, Fred, Jessica and Saulo get a push notification "WhatsApp PC stopped / quiet".

Optional settings in wa_agent.config.json (defaults shown):
  "poll_seconds": 0.5,  "heartbeat_seconds": 60,  "backlog_hours": 48
